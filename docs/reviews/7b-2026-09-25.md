## Most significant gaps

1. **Gap 2 is only fixed for the flat (single-level) diamond/loop case, not recursively through
   nested DECISIONs.** A DECISION nested inside one arm of an outer DECISION independently
   computes its own join without knowledge of the outer decision's `stop_ids`/`join_id`, so it can
   pre-emptively render the outer join inside only one arm — reproducing the *exact* original bug
   one level deeper. Confirmed by a hand-built synthetic probe (not covered by either of the
   implementer's two new tests, both single-level). Dormant on PID_171 (confirmed: Mark Exception's
   decisions are not nested this way, and the regenerated output is correct), but a real risk for
   the ~199 other automations this pipeline is meant to generalize to, per
   `.claude/rules/pad-pipeline-workflow.md`'s stated program scope.
2. **Gap 4's nested-paren regex only balances one level.** `Lower(Trim(ExceptionType()))` (or any
   two-level-nested call) fails to match at all and falls through untranslated. Not malformed the
   way the pre-fix bug was, and harmless on PID_171 (0 such cases exist in the real data), but a
   residual portability limit undisclosed in the implementer's summary.
3. Both gaps above are the kind of thing that would only surface on a different BP process with
   deeper nesting — neither corrupts or regresses this specific PID_0171 run, which is why the
   dimension scores above are high (88% overall) rather than blocked, but they are real,
   independently-reproduced defects in the generic mechanisms, not merely theoretical.

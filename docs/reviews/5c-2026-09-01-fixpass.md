(`src/flowsmith/generator/pad.py` ~L1938-1940) plus one new regression test
(`tests/generator/test_pad.py::test_coarse_block_fallback_to_error_block_when_no_exception_page`),
made in commit `657a42b` against the single defect the prior review (`5c-2026-09-01-head-verify.md`,

  3 coverage."
- Everything else previously identified by the head-verify review (recovery-content-between-

## Verdict

**Pass** for the narrow scope of this fix-pass. The specific, confirmed defect from
`5c-2026-09-01-head-verify.md` (6 dangling `GOTO` targets) is closed, verified independently by
regeneration and direct set-difference/per-scope inspection, with a genuine (non-trivial) regression

generality, and the broader Task 5c fidelity items already on record from the head-verify review)
are real but do not block accepting this fix-pass on its own declared scope.
